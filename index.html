Frequently asked questions

My progress is gone after reinstalling.
Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

I bought "Ad-Free Rewards" but don't have it anymore.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore next to Purchases.

How do I change the language?
The game follows your phone's language. You can also choose it in the game's Settings.
